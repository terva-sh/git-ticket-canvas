    Mockup limits and approval scope

    This new artifact extends the approved one-pen mockup without changing it or the approved pen policies. Review explicit rule order, specificity, overlap warnings, per-ticket winning/losing explanations, and reorder/removal previews. The ticket stays draft; this mockup is not yet approved.

    Fixed-size fixture cards use stable synthetic slots, not a proposed production algorithm. The demo has one board, button-based movement, a static ordinary frame, and a 900 ms simulated save. No drag, live updates, persistence, cross-board races, general rule editor, or production performance claims. Repeated-label and removal buttons expose routing cases without choosing the eventual authoring form. All labels are positive independent requirements, so equally specific rules can overlap even if their required sets share no label. Refresh resets everything.
